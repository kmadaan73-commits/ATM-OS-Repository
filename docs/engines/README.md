# ATM OS AI Engines

## Vision

AI Engines are the core intelligence layer of ATM OS.

Agents think.
Engines execute.

Every business process is powered by one or more AI Engines.

---

# Core Intelligence Engines

## 01. Decision Engine
Makes business decisions using rules, AI models and company policies.

---

## 02. Workflow Engine
Runs business workflows from start to finish.

---

## 03. Business Rules Engine
Applies all business rules automatically.

---

## 04. Human Approval Engine
Requests approval whenever AI confidence is low or company policy requires it.

---

## 05. Planning Engine
Creates daily, weekly, monthly and yearly execution plans.

---

## 06. Memory Engine
Stores long-term organisational knowledge, history and business context.

---

## 07. Learning Engine
Learns continuously from business outcomes and improves recommendations.

---

## 08. Prediction Engine
Predicts demand, sales, stock requirements and customer behaviour.

---

## 09. Risk Engine
Identifies financial, operational and business risks before execution.

---

## 10. Compliance Engine
Checks legal, tax, regulatory and internal policy compliance.

---

## 11. Audit Engine
Records every AI action with complete traceability.

---

## 12. Notification Engine
Sends alerts through Email, WhatsApp, SMS and Telegram.

---

## 13. Integration Engine
Connects ERP, CRM, APIs, databases and third-party software.

---

## 14. Automation Engine
Executes repetitive business tasks automatically.

---

## 15. Analytics Engine
Generates dashboards, KPIs and executive reports.

---

## 16. Pricing Engine
Calculates optimal selling prices and margins.

---

## 17. Procurement Engine
Optimises purchasing decisions using demand and supplier analysis.

---

## 18. Inventory Engine
Maintains healthy stock levels and prevents stock-outs or overstock.

---

## 19. Sales Intelligence Engine
Recommends products, offers and cross-selling opportunities.

---

## 20. Customer Intelligence Engine
Analyses customer behaviour, complaints and buying patterns.

---

## 21. Supplier Intelligence Engine
Evaluates suppliers using quality, delivery, pricing and reliability.

---

## 22. Finance Engine
Tracks profitability, cash flow, budgets and financial health.

---

## 23. Security Engine
Protects data, users, permissions and AI operations.

---

## 24. Multi-Business Engine
Loads business-specific knowledge based on the selected industry.

Examples:
- Medicine
- Electronics
- Cosmetics
- FMCG
- Chemicals
- Textile
- Agriculture
- Automobile

---

## 25. Orchestrator Engine
Coordinates all AI Agents and AI Engines across the organisation.

---
